01 — Where you stand

Know the body you’re starting from.

One photo. Awaken reads body fat, muscle, and a rank — then the distance to the physique you picked. A straight take, including the part that’s hard to hear.

The photo stays yours. It never goes on the board.

03 — This week

A week written for your actual life.

Gym or living room. What you can already lift. What hurts. How long you can stay. Home gets home sessions. The gym gets the gym. Same physique. Different tools.

Then it’s just today’s work. Finish it. See the muscles you hit. Log it. Come back tomorrow.

04 — Show up

Do the session. Keep what you built.

Open it. Train. The streak stays when you rest — recovery is part of the week, not a penalty for missing a day you were supposed to take off.

05 — Every 7 days

The week you lived decides the next one.

Awaken reads how those seven days went. Extra reps, the load climbs. You struggled, it comes down. You held the line, it stays. That’s how a body actually changes — the plan looks back at you.

About every two weeks, you scan again. This physique sits next to the last one. You stop arguing with the mirror.

Before you start

Straight answers.

Do I need a gym?

No. Say you train at home and list what you have. The sessions change. The physique you’re chasing doesn’t.

What happens if I rest?

The streak stays. Rest is part of the week. It doesn’t send you back to zero.

Who sees my scan?

Not the board. The photo is for your rank and your own before-and-after. Other people see your name, XP, streak, and rank — not your body.

Does the plan stay frozen?

No. Every 7 days Awaken reads the week and raises the load, lowers it, or holds it. About every 15 days you can scan again and see the change against the last one.

Is this medical advice?

No. Awaken is training. It is not a doctor. Work inside what your body can do, and stop if something hurts in a way training shouldn’t.
